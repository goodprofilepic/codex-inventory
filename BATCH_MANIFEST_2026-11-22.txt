Duplicate control

Cleaning and rights
Each text has the Reading Room metadata header: Title, Author, Date, Translator, Editor, Source Edition, Source URL, Description, Rights Status, Rights Notes. Footnote references and source endnote apparatus were removed where identified; authorial text and meaningful internal headings were retained. Modern copyrighted translations and uncertain attributions were excluded from the batch.
